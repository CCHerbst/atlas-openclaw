# Chris Herbst

## Role
Incoming Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at IP Corporation. This is a newly created role focused on three pillars:
1. **Technology Strategy**: setting the technology direction and roadmap for the enterprise
2. **Data to Business Value**: owning the pipeline from raw data to measurable business outcomes
3. **AI Direction and Application**: owning where AI is applied, what platforms are used, and building AI capability

The CIO (Mike Spencer) handles implementation and operations. The CTO sets direction. These are complementary roles, not overlapping.
